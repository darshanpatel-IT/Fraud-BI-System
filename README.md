# Fraud BI System

An end-to-end fraud analytics and business intelligence solution built using MySQL, Python, Power BI, and DAX.

## Project Overview

This project simulates a digital payment ecosystem and analyzes transactions to identify fraud patterns, merchant performance, and customer behavior.

## Dataset Scale

- 5,000 Customers
- 500 Merchants
- 50,000 Transactions
- 1,500 Fraud Cases

## Tech Stack

- MySQL
- Python
- Power BI
- DAX

## Business Insights

### Executive Dashboard Insights
- Fraud transactions account for approximately 3% of all transactions.
- Successful transactions contribute more than 90% of total transaction volume.
- Transaction failures account for approximately 7.8% of all transactions.

### Fraud Analysis Insights
- High-risk transactions represent a small portion of fraud cases but require immediate investigation.
- Certain payment methods show higher fraud concentrations.
- Specific cities contribute disproportionately to fraud activity.

### Merchant Performance Insights
- Merchant revenue is highly concentrated among a small number of merchants.
- Merchant fraud rates vary significantly across merchants.
- Some merchants maintain success rates above 90%.

### Customer Insights
- Customer spending behavior differs significantly across customer segments.
- Payment preferences vary between customer groups.
- Fraud involvement is concentrated among a subset of customers.

## Business Recommendations

### Fraud Prevention
- Introduce additional verification checks for high-risk transactions.
- Apply stricter monitoring for transactions originating from high-risk devices and locations.
- Implement real-time fraud scoring for suspicious activities.

### Payment Security
- Increase monitoring for payment methods with higher fraud rates.
- Apply adaptive authentication for risky payment channels.

### Merchant Risk Management
- Monitor merchants with high failure rates and abnormal fraud patterns.
- Introduce merchant risk scoring and periodic reviews.

### Customer Risk Management
- Track customers involved in repeated fraud incidents.
- Introduce behavioral analytics to identify unusual spending patterns.

### Operational Improvements
- Build automated fraud alerts for critical risk transactions.
- Enable daily executive monitoring through Power BI dashboards.
